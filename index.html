How it works

From a spin of the globe to a business card

  1. Spin and click a region

    Rotate the globe and click a US state, Canadian province or territory, or European country. The globe zooms in and the list panel opens.

  2. Browse business cards

    Each card shows the business name, category, address, phone, website, and email where published, plus a fit rating.

  3. Filter by category

    Narrow the list by business type, minimum fit, or a search term, and sort by fit or name.

  4. Personal outreach panel

    Open a business to see editable email and call scripts for one-to-one outreach. Nothing is sent automatically; one recipient at a time.

Where the listings come from

Business listings come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), a public, community-maintained map. No bought or scraped lists are used.

Privacy by design

  • Emails are hidden for European businesses.
  • Only contact details a business has published publicly are shown.
  • Personal, one-at-a-time outreach only. Opt-outs are honored the same day.
What Lead Globe is for

Finding businesses that may be a fit, one at a time

Lead Globe helps an independent DataEarners partner find businesses that may be a fit for the program. It locates businesses across the US, Canada, and Europe with their public contact details (phone, website, and email where available; emails are hidden for Europe). From there, the partner reaches out personally, one business at a time, to introduce the free Data Asset Profile through the invite link.

Lead Globe is an independent partner tool by Robert Walker, not an official DataEarners or Mode product.

  • Find fit businesses

    Browse by region and category to surface businesses that may be a fit.

  • Review contacts & fit

    Check each card’s public phone, website, and email where available, plus its fit rating.

  • Personal, one-to-one outreach

    Select a business and get a ready-to-send outreach email and call script tailored to its industry, which you personalize and send yourself, one business at a time.

  • Respectful by default

    Opt-outs are honored. No bulk email, and no bought or scraped lists.

For business owners

Explore whether your company’s data could be licensed

Through the DataEarners program, a business can request a free Data Asset Profile: a look at the business systems your company already runs. That profile may lead to a written offer from a data licensing company (Mode) to license consented, de-identified company data that is used to build AI.

Using the globe or viewing listings does not involve any payment. Whether an offer is made, and its terms, is decided by the licensing company and is subject to its review.

  • Free Data Asset Profile
  • Consented & de-identified
  • Written offer, if any, from the licensing company
