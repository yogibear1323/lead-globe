Interactive 3D business globe

Search businesses around the world with a spin of the globe.

Lead Globe is an interactive 3D globe. Spin it, click any US state, Canadian province or territory, or European country, and a list of local businesses opens right away, with contact details (phone, website, and email where available) on each card.

How it works

From a spin of the globe to a business card

  1. Spin and click a region

    Rotate the globe and click a US state, Canadian province or territory, or European country. The globe zooms in and the list panel opens.

  2. Browse business cards

    Each card shows the business name, category, address, phone, website, and email where published, plus a fit rating.

  3. Filter by category

    Narrow the list by business type, minimum fit, or a search term, and sort by fit or name.

  4. Personal outreach panel

    Open a business to see editable email and call scripts for one-to-one outreach. Nothing is sent automatically; one recipient at a time.

Where the listings come from

Business listings come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), a public, community-maintained map. No bought or scraped lists are used.

Privacy by design

  • Emails are hidden for European businesses.
  • Only contact details a business has published publicly are shown.
  • Personal, one-at-a-time outreach only. Opt-outs are honored the same day.
